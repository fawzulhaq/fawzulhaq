How to export your LinkedIn data (takes ~10 minutes):

1. Go to LinkedIn → Me → Settings → Data Privacy → Get a copy of your data

2. Select "The works" or choose: Profile, Positions, Education, Skills, Certifications

3. Click Request archive — LinkedIn emails you a ZIP file (usually within minutes to 24 hours)

4. Download the ZIP and drop it in the box below ↓
